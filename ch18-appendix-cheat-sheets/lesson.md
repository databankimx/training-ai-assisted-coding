# Appendix: Cheat Sheets

Each chapter in this course has a longer lesson, with examples and a worked exercise. Once you've finished a chapter, the lesson is the right place to learn the idea. This appendix is the place to review it.

Each sheet is one or two pages. It holds the questions to ask, the checks to run, and the mistake that shows up most often. Nothing in a sheet is new. A sheet points back to the chapter it came from, so you can return to the full explanation when a point isn't clear.
