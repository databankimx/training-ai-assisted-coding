# Knowing When to Stop

Fifteen chapters have been about using the assistant well. This one is about using it less, at the right moments, and about recognizing the moment when continuing with it costs more than it returns. A developer who can't stop an assistant session is as limited as one who can't start one.

The chapter is about judgment, so its steps are mostly text with worked examples. The course's final project, the capstone, is in the next chapter.

## What you'll do

1. **Where assistance is most valuable.** The kinds of work where an assistant pays back its cost quickly, and the kinds where it doesn't.
2. **Stop conditions.** The signs that a session has stopped converging, and what to do instead.
3. **From prototype to production.** Why a prototype the assistant helped build needs a different process before it reaches users.
4. **Skill and unassisted practice.** How to keep your own skills sharp, and why the habit matters for the review.
5. **A decision framework.** A short set of questions to ask before each task.

The summary has three quizzes.
