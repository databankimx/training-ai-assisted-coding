# Cheat Sheet: Data and Accounts

*From Chapter 2. Use before anything goes into a prompt.*

## Four answers

- **Allowed.** Nothing identifies a person, client, system, or credential, and no company information is included.
- **Allowed after removing details.** The problem can be shared once identifiers are replaced with placeholders.
- **Not allowed.** Customer data, credentials, personal information, client code, or product code, which can't be made safe by removing details.
- **Ask first.** Company information that isn't obviously sensitive, or anything you can't classify with confidence.

## The questions, in order

1. Does it contain a name, identifier, or contact detail for a person?
2. Does it contain a credential, key, token, or connection string?
3. Does it describe a client, customer data, or client code?
4. Does it contain DataBank product code, a proprietary algorithm, or an unreleased plan?
5. Does it describe an internal system, process, or decision that nobody has cleared?

A "yes" to any question stops the paste until the item is classified.

## Accounts

- Company work uses a company-provided account, through an approved tool.
- Learning on invented data can use the free tier.
- Check the account name and the browser profile before each session.
- A new tool needs your manager's approval and an IT ticket before use.

## Common mistake

Treating published material as cleared when the policy asks for Information Security's clearance. Ask first if unsure.
