# Capstone

This is the course's final project. It uses the workflow from the earlier chapters on one task, in two languages, and it ends with the review a team lead would expect. The work is yours. The brief gives you the spec, the acceptance examples, and the rubric, and it asks you to make every other decision yourself, with the assistant as a tool and not as the author.

The project is a loan reminder module for a small library. It's deliberately close to the fines module you already know, with one new rule and one new kind of error, so the new parts stand out.

## What you'll do

1. **The loan reminder module.** Read the brief, the spec, the acceptance table, and the rubric, then deliver the nine parts listed in the brief.

The capstone is graded against behavior and against the quality of your review. It doesn't reward the amount of code you produced.
